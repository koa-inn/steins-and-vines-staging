Squamish's Ferment-on-Premises and Homebrew Supply Shop

Squamish's ferment-on-premises destination for handcrafted wine. Choose from a wide selection of premium wine kits, and we'll guide you through every step—from grape to glass. You start the batch, we handle the heavy lifting, and you walk away with bottles you're proud to pour.

What is U-Ferment?

U-Ferment (also called U-Vin or U-Brew) lets you make wine on our premises using professional equipment and our expertise. It's legal, it's affordable, and the results rival what you'd find on any store shelf. You pick a kit, start fermentation with us, and return in a few weeks to bottle your finished wine—approximately ~29 bottles (23 litres) per batch, no experience needed. Because you make it yourself on our premises, your batch is not subject to liquor taxes.

How It Works

Making your own wine in Squamish takes four steps. Wine kits start at $190 and yield 28–30 bottles; cider kits start at $125.

  1. 1

    Choose your kit

    Browse wine kits, ciders, and fruit-forward blends online, or drop in to see what's on the shelf.

  2. 2

    We start your batch

    Book a 20-minute appointment and we'll walk you through starting fermentation on our equipment.

  3. 3

    Fermentation on-site

    Your batch sits in our temperature-controlled facility for 4–8 weeks. No monitoring on your end.

  4. 4

    Come back to bottle

    About 45 minutes to cap, cork, and apply custom labels. Take home bottles with your name on them.

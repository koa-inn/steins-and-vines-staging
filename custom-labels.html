About Our Label Materials

Waterproof

Laminated and dishwasher-safe. Won’t peel, smudge, or fade — perfect for anything that goes in a fridge, cooler, or gets handled a lot.

Paper

Budget-friendly with vibrant colour reproduction. Best for dry environments like pantry items, gifts, or event favours.

Design Guidelines

File Format

PNG or JPG preferred. WEBP also accepted. 300 DPI or higher at the final label size — higher resolution is welcome; anything lower may look soft or pixelated.

Bleed & Safe Area

Add 1/8" (3 mm) bleed on all sides. Keep important text and logos at least 1/4" (6 mm) from the trim edge.

Colour Mode

CMYK is ideal for print accuracy. RGB files are accepted and will be converted.

Print Dimensions

See the pricing table above for all available label sizes. Contact us if you need a custom size.

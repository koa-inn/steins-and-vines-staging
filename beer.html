What It Is

The same ferment-on-premises setup you know from our wine kits — now for beer. The difference is that we do the brewing for you. A brew day runs several hours, but you don’t need to be here for it — your only job that day takes about ten minutes. Beer batches are booked ahead rather than taken as walk-ins, so it starts with getting on the list.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Join the waitlist

    Leave us your email, or give us a call. Beer batches are booked ahead, and we work through the list in order.

  2. 2

    We plan it with you

    When your turn comes up we’ll reach out and set up a consult — over the phone or here in the shop — to settle the details and lock in your recipe.

  3. 3

    We brew it

    A brew day runs several hours and we handle it. All we need from you that day is about ten minutes to pitch the yeast. Want to be hands-on for the whole brew? You’re very welcome — plenty of people are.

  4. 4

    Come in and can it

    We look after your batch while it ferments — typically about 3 weeks for ales and about 5 weeks for lagers, counted from your brew day. Each recipe’s card shows its own estimate, and we’ll confirm your exact packaging day as your batch gets close. Most of our beer gets canned, and canning is done with you here — about half an hour for a standard 30 L batch. Prefer bottles? Crown tops and flip-tops are welcome; they just take a little longer.

What’s Included

Beer is priced per recipe, so what you pay depends on what you choose to brew. We’ll go through the options and give you a price at your consult.

Whatever you pick, the price covers the lot: ingredients, all equipment and full use of the studio, fermentation and monitoring throughout, canning and lids, and both the maker’s and materials fees. Nothing extra gets added on canning day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to be there for the whole brew day?

No — and this is the part most people get wrong about brewing with us. A brew day runs several hours, but we do the brewing. The only step that needs you is pitching the yeast, which takes under ten minutes, and we’ll agree a time for it at your consult. If you’d like to be there for the whole thing and get stuck in, you’re very welcome — it’s just never required.

So when do I actually need to come in?

Twice, and neither is a full day. Once on brew day to pitch the yeast — about ten minutes, at a time we agree in advance. Then again to package your beer once it’s ready: that session is the one you do need to be present for, and canning a standard 30 L batch takes about half an hour. Your consult can happen over the phone, so it needn’t be a trip at all.

Can I have bottles instead of cans?

Yes. We can most of our beer — it is quicker and it keeps better — but if you would rather have crown-top or flip-top bottles, you are very welcome to. Just allow a bit more time at your packaging session, as bottling is slower than canning. Let us know at your consult so we can book the right amount of time.

Do I need brewing experience?

No. We handle the brewing, and we’ll walk you through the part you do. Most people who brew with us have never brewed before.

How long until it’s ready?

It depends on the style — typically about 3 weeks for ales, about 5 weeks for lagers, counted from your brew day. Each recipe’s card on this page shows its own estimate. We’ll confirm the exact day you come in to package it as your batch gets close.

Can I bring my own recipe?

Get in touch. We’ve got a range of recipes to choose from, and we’re happy to hear what you have in mind.

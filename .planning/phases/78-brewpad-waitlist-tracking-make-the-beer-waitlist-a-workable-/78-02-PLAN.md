<objective>
Make the `Waitlist` sheet row authoritative for a beer signup and open the admin proxy for BrewPad's two waitlist actions.

Purpose: today a MailerLite outage returns 503 and turns away a customer the shop could serve, while a successful signup leaves no record anyone can read. After this plan the sheet write is the thing that decides success, MailerLite is best-effort, and marketing drift is recorded on the row instead of vanishing into a log line.

Output: `POST /api/waitlist` writes the row blockingly and fails closed on that write; `get_waitlist` and `update_waitlist_status` are reachable from BrewPad through `/api/batch/admin-proxy`; two new middleware test files prove both.
</objective>

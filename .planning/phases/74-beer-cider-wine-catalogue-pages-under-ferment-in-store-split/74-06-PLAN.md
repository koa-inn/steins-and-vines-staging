<objective>
Wire the two new pages into the page dispatch, fix a live shipped bug found during planning, rebuild the production bundles, and run the release gate.

Purpose: this is the plan that makes the phase actually work in a browser. `js/modules/13-init.js` decides what runs per page, and it currently has no branch for `wine` or `beer` at all. Output: a working `/wine` and `/beer`, a fixed waitlist, regenerated bundles, green suites on both sides, the D-08 content pass, and a human verification of all three surfaces.

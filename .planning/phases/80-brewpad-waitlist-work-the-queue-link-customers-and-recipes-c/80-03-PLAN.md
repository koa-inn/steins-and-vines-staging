<planner_decisions>
- **`position` semantics (matching plan 80-01's storage contract):** a positive integer = "this
  row's target 1-based rank in the merged queue". Empty/absent = unpinned. The merge is a pure
  render-time splice; nothing on disk is ever renumbered, so pinning one row cannot touch another
  row's `signed_up_at` (the bar the discretion note sets).
- **Pin control renders on EVERY row regardless of status** (UI-SPEC Phase-Specific Decision 5).
  For non-`waiting` rows, which `computeWaitlistQueuePositions` never numbers, the `#` cell shows
  `📌 —` when pinned and plain `—` when unpinned. This is flagged for owner confirmation in plan
  80-06's checkpoint; if overturned, the change is a one-line render guard.
- **`beer.html` is NOT touched** (D-14). "We work through the list in order" stays true because the
  queue is chronological unless staff deliberately intervene.
- **Customer, Recipes and Contact cells are display-only in this plan.** Their triggers arrive in
  80-04 (link customer, attach recipe) and 80-05 (contact button). Do not render a control whose
  handler does not exist yet — the tab must be fully functional at this plan's commit.
</planner_decisions>

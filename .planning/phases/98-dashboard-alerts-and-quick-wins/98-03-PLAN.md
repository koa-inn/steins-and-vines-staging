(weekEnd via toISOString) stays UTC; record it in the SUMMARY as a separate follow-up, do not change it here.

(owner-approved, RESEARCH Q2). The SUMMARY must give both a heading of their own, "Behaviour changes beyond D-06", with
before/after and the reason for each. Plan 98-06 UAT checks (a) after 4 pm PT.

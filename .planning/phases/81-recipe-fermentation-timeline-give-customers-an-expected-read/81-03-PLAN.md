<objective>
Render the derived figure on the public beer recipe card as "Ready in / about N weeks / from
brew day", and rewrite the two `beer.html` passages that still deflect the question to the
consult.

Purpose: this is the entire customer-visible deliverable of the phase. D-07 puts the figure
in a second `.price-col` beside the price so the customer's two questions — what does it cost
and when do I get it — sit together. D-06 makes the phrase carry its own start point so the
card cannot be misread when screenshotted or shared. D-08 rewrites both `beer.html` passages
because leaving "we'll give you a timeline at your consult" on a page whose cards now state
weeks makes the page argue with itself.

Output: a `fermentTimeDisplay()` helper, a conditional second column in `buildRecipeCard`,
~10 lines of additive CSS, two rewritten `beer.html` passages, rebuilt bundles, and new test
cases.
</objective>
